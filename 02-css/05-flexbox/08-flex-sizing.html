08 - Flex Sizing (Grow, Shrink, Basis)

1. flex-grow (Proportional Growth)

Item 2 grows twice as fast as Item 1 and Item 3.

grow: 1
grow: 2
grow: 1

2. flex-basis (Initial Main Size)

Sets initial default size of a flex item before growing/shrinking.

basis: 100px
basis: 200px

3. flex-shrink (Shrinking Rate)

Item 1 (shrink: 0) refuses to shrink, while Item 2 and 3 shrink to fit container.

shrink: 0
shrink: 1
shrink: 2
